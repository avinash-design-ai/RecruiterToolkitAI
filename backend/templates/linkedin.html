📖 How to Use LinkedIn Automation V2

Follow the steps below to run the LinkedIn automation and export your search results.

STEP 1: Enter Search Details

Open Recruiter Toolkit V2 and enter the search information you want the automation to use.

  • Enter the Company Name.
  • Enter the Location.
  • Select the desired Maximum Profiles.

Click Run Search to start the LinkedIn automation workflow.

STEP 2: LinkedIn Login and Verification

On the first run, the automation opens LinkedIn in a browser window.

Sign in to your LinkedIn account and complete any required authentication or verification.

Important: Complete the login or verification in the browser opened by the application. Once authentication is completed, the automation continues automatically.

STEP 3: Automated LinkedIn Search and Profile Extraction

After LinkedIn authentication is complete, Recruiter Toolkit V2 automatically applies the Company and Location entered in the application.

The automation opens the available employee profiles and processes them one by one.

Publicly visible profile information and available email IDs are extracted from the profile pages. Email addresses that are discovered but do not appear to belong to the profile owner are kept separately as Linked Email IDs.

STEP 4: Save Your Search Results

Once the search is completed, Recruiter Toolkit V2 displays the number of profiles collected and automatically opens a Save As dialog.

Choose your preferred location, such as your Downloads or Desktop folder, and save the results as a CSV file.

The application suggests a V2 filename based on the company and location used for the search.

STEP 5: Review the Final CSV Output

Open the saved CSV file in Excel to review the collected LinkedIn profile information.

Recruiter Toolkit V2 keeps publicly visible email information organized into separate fields, including Email, Email Source, and Linked Email ID.

This helps distinguish an email associated with the profile from another email address that may simply appear elsewhere on the publicly visible profile.
